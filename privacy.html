Privacy Policy

Party Proof, by Thomas Innovative Technologies. Draft, November 2026; final before launch.

The short version: Party Proof keeps your game on your phone. No accounts, ads or analytics, and photos never leave the phone that took them. Game events (player names, challenges completed and scores) are shared with the other players in your game. Weekend games also use a relay that can't read what it carries and deletes it 48 hours after the game.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names, boards, scores, trivia answers, secret missions and your recap.
  • Photos you take or choose as proof. They're resized, location and camera details are removed, and they're saved only inside the app until you save them to your photo library yourself.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you add photo proof. You can say no and still play. The app never records audio.

Playing together

Phones in the same game share game events so everyone sees the same scoreboard: display names, which challenges are done, points, trivia answers and the squares, questions and missions the host chose. Photos are never shared.

Nearby: phones close together connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Apple's encrypted peer-to-peer technology. iPhone asks once for "Local Network" permission.

Over the internet: when there's a signal, game events are passed along live through Ably, a realtime messaging service. Ably keeps each event for at most two minutes so a phone that briefly lost signal can catch up. Like any internet service, Ably processes your phone's network address to deliver the data (Ably privacy).

Weekend games: a small relay we run on Cloudflare holds the game's score records so a phone that was closed can catch up. Records are encrypted on the phone with a key that only the players have, so we can't read them. Games and players are identified only by random codes. Everything for a game is deleted automatically 48 hours after it ends, and we don't keep logs of network addresses.

Secret missions are encrypted so only the player they belong to can see them until the recap.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. When a host with a Host Pass starts a game, their phone shares Apple's proof of purchase with the phones in that game so the full game unlocks for everyone. For weekend games, our relay checks that proof with Apple once and keeps only the fact that the game is unlocked.

Reports

If you tap Report, the app opens an email with the game code and the reported text. You decide whether to send it. If you do, we receive that email, including your email address, and use it only to review the report.

App updates

When the app opens, it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for small improvements. This shares your phone's network address and the app's version, nothing about your game.

Age

Party Proof is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: support@bthomasconsulting.com.
