Ground rules

Terms of use for Party Proof, by Thomas Innovative Technologies. Draft, November 2026; final before launch.

Everyone agrees to these when they first open the app. They keep the game fun for the whole crew and for the people you meet along the way.

1. You're 18 or older

Party Proof is for adults. Some optional challenges are cheeky.

2. Ask before you snap

Only photograph people who say yes, and stop if anyone asks you to. Never take photos of anyone undressed, in a bathroom or changing room, or of children you don't know. Respect the rules of the places you visit.

3. Keep it kind

No hate, harassment or bullying in names, squares or questions. The app filters hateful words, and anything that gets through can be reported or blocked.

4. Hosts own what they write

Party Proof's own challenges never involve drinking. Squares, questions and missions a host writes are the host's choice, and the host is responsible for them and for the game they run. If you drink, drink responsibly, and never drive afterwards.

5. Stay safe and legal

No challenge is worth getting hurt, breaking the law or bothering strangers. Skip anything that doesn't feel right. You are responsible for what you do while playing.

6. Your photos are yours

Photos stay on your phone. Share them only with people who are in them and are happy for you to.

7. Reports, blocks and removals

Any player can block or report another player from the scoreboard, and the host can remove a player from the game. Reports come to us by email and we review every one within 24 hours. We may stop supporting games or players that break these rules.

8. The usual fine print

Party Proof is provided as is. Apple's standard licensed application end user license agreement also applies. Questions: support@bthomasconsulting.com.
