Report something in a game

Tap Report on any square, question, mission or player name you think is offensive. It opens an email to us with the game code and the reported text, which you send from your own phone. We review every report within 24 hours. You can also block a player on your phone, and the host can remove a player from the game.

Common questions

Do guests have to pay? No. Guests always play free. The host's Host Pass unlocks the full board, more players and weekend games for everyone in that game.

How do guests join? The host shares an invite link or shows a QR code. Phones in the same room can also find the game nearby.

Does it work with no signal? Yes. Phones in the same room link over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so the board, scoreboard, trivia and missions keep working.

Where do my photos go? Nowhere. They stay on the phone that took them, and you can save them to your photo library from the recap. To collect everyone's photos, use an iCloud shared album or AirDrop.

Can I delete my data? Everything is on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it. For weekend games, the encrypted score records are deleted from our relay automatically 48 hours after the game ends.
